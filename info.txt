ICBM MOD "Less Aggressive AI"

Version "2.4.0"

Description "AI factions need a reason to go to war. A scripted motive layer scores every rival separately - a weak spot on the border it can actually see (intel matters), provocations that accumulate, a rival busy elsewhere, occupied land - and opens a war against that faction only when opportunity and reason coincide; a long standoff slowly wears down restraint. Territorial wars are fought in full, punitive ones as limited strikes. Ceasefires and treaties are respected. Nuclear weapons are a last resort: tactical only to hold land worth holding, strategic only when the threat is existential, either in kind. Vanilla build logic. Iron Curtain map; optional colourblind palette."
