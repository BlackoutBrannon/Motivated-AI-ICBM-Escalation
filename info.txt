ICBM MOD "Motivated AI"

Version "2.5.0"

Description "AI factions need a reason to go to war. A scripted motive layer scores every rival separately - a weak spot on the border it can actually see (intel matters), provocations that accumulate, a rival busy elsewhere, occupied land - and opens a war against that faction only when opportunity and reason coincide; a long standoff slowly wears down restraint. Opportunity alone is never enough. Territorial wars are fought in full, punitive ones as limited strikes. Incidents are not wars but they are a powder keg: a stray kill is shrugged off by a faction with no other grudge and tips one that already has a reason. Wars that go nowhere for 72 minutes end in a ceasefire. Ceasefires and treaties are respected. Nuclear weapons are a last resort: tactical only to hold land worth holding, strategic only when the threat is existential - a fifth of the country lost and held, or a vital region about to fall - or in kind. Vanilla build logic. Iron Curtain map; optional colourblind palette."

ChangeLog "2.5.0 - Renamed from Less Aggressive AI. Every behavioural change now verified in a four-hour hands-off run plus a forced test: standoffs maturing into reasoned wars, a stalemate ceasefire negotiated by both sides, tactical and strategic releases both earned, no cascade, four factions never dragged in at all. Fixed: the peace cooldown expired on the evaluation that created it (the engine appends both operands of `set += (a + b)` instead of adding them); escalating a war left a duplicate in the released set, which over-charged the multi-front penalty and could survive a ceasefire. Changed: a vital region about to fall authorises strategic immediately, gated on invaders outnumbering defenders rather than on a hold timer - nobody waits out a timer while the capital is being taken; a destroyed unit is a heavy grievance rather than an automatic war; an uninvited border crossing is a grievance, not an attack; aircraft over their own territory no longer provoke; EMP is tactical, not strategic; stalemates wind down after 72 minutes instead of two hours, so peace drains the map faster than war fills it; the warm-up is 10 minutes instead of 30, since the scoring now prevents an opening war on its own and 30 meant an AI would absorb a sustained attack without ever responding. 2.4.0 - First fully autonomous long run verified: ~90 min of peace, then one reasoned NATO-Pact war with bloc responses, tactical release and use, no strategic, no cascades, partnerships forming on their own. Added: ceasefire/treaty respect with a one-hour cooldown, standoff pressure (5%/eval after 90 min within reach), territorial existential trigger (20% of regions or one worth 25%), tactical trigger needs a region worth 5% or two regions lost, overflights count in the air-defence zone, partners exempt, geneva-category nuclear doctrine scoped FOR ME, optional colourblind palette. 2.3.0 - Nuclear doctrine (tactical when failing to hold a region, strategic when existential, either in kind; salted only in kind). Mutual defence: alliance wars fought in full. Performance: bucketed scans, staggered evaluations. 2.2.0 - Verified end to end in play: motive -> declaration -> engine invasion (Hamburg captured 3.5 min after the Pact declared on a distracted NATO). Fixed the same-tick re-block freeze, delayed leash restore, motive-driven war kind, grievance accumulation, cascade damping. 2.1.0 - Decided wars open with AttackFaction (invasions happen), retaliation is rolling LimitedAttack, garrison back to vanilla (stricter one is an opt-in build switch). 2.0.0 - First fully verified end-to-end version: per-region land grab with reinforcement discount, intel-coverage gating, warm-up period, retaliation on attack, leash re-assertion, opportunity-alone-is-never-enough rule, debug lines for a watched faction. 1.x - initial script and garrison work."
